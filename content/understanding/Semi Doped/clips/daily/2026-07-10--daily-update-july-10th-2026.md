### Meta AI Chip Enters Production in September, $10B Canada Data Center Announced

Meta plans to begin production of its in-house AI chip in September as part of an effort to double computing capacity, Reuters reported. The company is also investing $10 billion in a data center in Alberta, Canada — its first in the country — as it continues expanding its global infrastructure fleet. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/meta-to-put-ai-chip-into-production-in-september-report.html), [Bloomberg.com](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-08/meta-to-build-first-data-center-in-canada-expanding-global-fleet))

### Apollo’s $35B AI Chip Credit Facility Enters Secondary Market

Apollo Global Management’s $35 billion AI chip credit facility has begun trading in the secondary market. The deal, structured to finance AI chip purchases, represents one of the largest private credit arrangements tied to semiconductor infrastructure. ([Bloomberg.com](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-09/apollo-s-35-billion-ai-chip-credit-deal-is-set-to-begin-trading))

> _**Vik:** This is the largest private credit deal on record for financing Broadcom and Anthropic AI buildout. Of the planned $35B, about $15B should be available for trading by 2027._
> 
> _**Austin:** “The borrower in the transaction is a special purpose vehicle that is buying custom chips developed by Google and Broadcom, and leasing them out to Anthropic.”_
> 
>  _Interesting. Anthropic not buying TPUs. Middleman buys TPUs. Anthropic just rents them._

### CXMT Sets July 16 Subscription Date for $4.3B Shanghai IPO

ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT), China’s leading DRAM maker based in Hefei, has set July 16 as the subscription date for its Shanghai Star Market IPO, which is expected to raise at least 29.5 billion yuan ($4.3 billion). The offering would rank as the second-largest listing on the Star Market and the largest Chinese IPO in four years. CXMT held its initial price consultation Monday ahead of the subscription opening. ([South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/tech/article/3359955/chinas-top-dram-maker-sets-date-us43b-shanghai-ipo-amid-memory-boom))

> _**Vik:** No better time in history for a memory company to IPO._

### SK Hynix Prices ADR Offering at $149, Seven Times Oversubscribed

SK Hynix priced its U.S. American depositary receipt offering at $149 per ADR, a level 3.1% above its Korea close, with the deal drawing seven times more demand than available shares. SK Group Chairman Chey Tae-won traveled to New York for the Nasdaq listing ceremony, where the company presented its AI memory growth strategy to global investors. ([Bloomberg Tech](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-07-09/sk-hynix-us-listing-is-seven-times-oversubscribed-video), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/sk-hynix-us-listing-more-than-seven-times-oversubscribed-source-says-2026-07-09/))

### Rapidus Sets ¥3.5M 2nm Wafer Price to Undercut TSMC

Japan’s Rapidus is targeting a price of ¥3.5 million (approximately $24,000) per 2nm wafer, according to TrendForce — a level designed to undercut TSMC’s comparable node pricing. Rapidus is aiming to begin risk production at its Chitose, Hokkaido fab in 2025 and volume production in 2027, positioning the pricing as a way to attract customers away from TSMC’s leading-edge offerings. ([TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/07/09/news-japans-rapidus-takes-on-tsmc-with-aggressive-2nm-pricing-reportedly-targeting-up-to-%C2%A53-5m-per-wafer/))

> _**Vik:** Reportedly, TSMC 2nm and Intel 18A have a price of $30,000 per wafer. Rapidus pricing lower to attract customers._
> 
> _**Austin:** Makes sense. Have to attract customers somehow. Is a 20% discount enough?_

### nLIGHT Wins $627M Military Laser Weapon Contract

nLIGHT has been awarded a $627 million contract to develop the Joint Laser Weapon System (JLWS) for the US military. ### Nexchip Shares Rise After $891M Hong Kong IPO

Chinese chipmaker Nexchip completed an $891 million IPO on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange, with shares rising on the first day of trading. The Hefei-based company, which specializes in display driver ICs and other analog chips, is one of China’s largest domestic foundries operating at the 28nm node and below. ([Bloomberg Tech](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-09/nexchip-set-to-debut-in-hong-kong-after-890-million-listing))

> _**Vik:** Nexchip is China’s 3rd largest pure-play foundry after SMIC and Hua Hong. They make 40nm to 150nm chips which are great for analog chips._

### GigaDevice H1 Net Profit Surges 1,099% on Memory Shortage

GigaDevice Semiconductor reported preliminary H1 2026 net profit of CNY 6.9 billion, up 1,099% year-on-year, reflecting a broader memory stock rally driven by chip shortages and higher average selling prices. The Beijing-based company, which designs NOR flash and DRAM products, saw shares rise on both its A-share and H-share listings following the announcement. GigaDevice did not provide a revenue figure in the preliminary disclosure. ([Moomoo](https://www.moomoo.com/news/post/72746260/gigadevice-semiconductor-sees-h1-profit-soaring-more-than-11-fold), [digitimes](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260710VL211/profit-gigadevice-2026-earnings-investment.html), [finance.biggo.com](https://finance.biggo.com/news/3e7c78a3-d37a-466b-87b2-17826292ea6d))

### Technoprobe Stock Surges 330% on AI Packaging Demand

European probe card supplier Technoprobe has seen its stock surge 330%, driven by its role in the supply chain for Nvidia’s AI chips. The company provides probe cards and handling systems for advanced packaging and wafer-level testing. The stock’s performance highlights a market shift toward valuing backend packaging and test equipment, which are critical bottlenecks for AI accelerator production. ([Bloomberg Tech](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-10/technoprobe-s-330-surge-makes-it-a-european-ai-stock-standout))
