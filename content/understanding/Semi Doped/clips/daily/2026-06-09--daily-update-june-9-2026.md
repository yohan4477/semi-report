### **Alphabet contracts Intel to fabricate three million chips**

Alphabet has tapped Intel to manufacture roughly three million of its in-house designed chips, according to a report from The Information cited by Reuters. The arrangement marks a foundry win for Intel, which has been working to expand its contract manufacturing business and attract large customers for its fabrication services. Alphabet, the parent company of Google, develops custom silicon including its Tensor Processing Units and Axion CPUs for use across its data center and consumer product lines. Neither Alphabet nor Intel has publicly confirmed the deal, and specific terms, process node, and timing of the production run were not disclosed in the report. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/google-nvidia-consider-intel-backup-chip-manufacturer-information-reports-2026-06-08/), [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/google-nvidia-consider-intel-backup-chip-manufacturer))

### **Marvell joins S &P 500, Astera Labs flagged as candidate**

Marvell Technology shares jumped after the chipmaker was added to the S&P 500 index, replacing a departing constituent in the latest quarterly rebalance. Barron’s flagged Astera Labs, Bloom Energy, Alnylam Pharmaceuticals, and Reddit as candidates for upcoming additions to the benchmark, citing market capitalization and liquidity thresholds that each name now clears. Astera Labs, which supplies connectivity silicon for AI servers, has seen its market cap expand alongside hyperscaler infrastructure spending since its 2024 IPO. Index inclusion typically triggers buying from passive funds tracking the S&P 500, which manage trillions in assets. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/marvell-shares-jump-after-chipmaker-wins-spot-sp-500-2026-06-08/)

### **SpaceX unveils TeraFab plan, Nvidia-powered AI1 satellites**

SpaceX CEO Elon Musk outlined plans for a roughly 100 million-square-foot chip factory, dubbed TeraFab, aimed at producing a terawatt of AI compute capacity per year — ten times the footprint of Tesla’s Gigafactory Texas. Musk said the buildout is needed to supply SpaceX’s AI1 satellites, which carry computing modules, a 70-meter solar array, and terabit laser links routed through Starlink. The current reference design uses Nvidia Rubin or GB300 chips, with TPU reference designs also planned. Musk projected an annualized launch rate of one gigawatt of orbital compute by the end of 2027, scaling exponentially from there.
