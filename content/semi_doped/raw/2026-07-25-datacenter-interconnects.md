Austin and Vik break down the single biggest bottleneck in modern AI: data movement. They explain the three tiers of datacenter networking, the intense debate between copper and optics for different applications, and why the future of high-speed interconnects might be co-packaged with the chip itself. 🤖

* The three types of datacenter networks: scale up, scale out, and scale across
* Copper vs. optics for different reaches
* Pluggable optics and the move toward co-packaged optics (CPO)
* Nvidia’s NVLink vs. open standards like UALink
* The role of DSPs and transceivers in optical links
* Networking speeds from 400G to 1.6T
