Vik talks with Ilan Sever, Chief Memory Architect at Weebit Nano, about the ubiquitous but often-overlooked non-volatile memory (NVM) inside most chips. Ilan explains why the long-standing solution, embedded flash, stops scaling at advanced nodes like 22nm. He details how Weebit’s Resistive RAM (ReRAM) provides a low-cost, high-endurance alternative that is particularly well-suited for demanding markets like automotive.

**Things we cover:**

  * The role of non-volatile memory in MCUs and analog chips

  * Why embedded flash stops scaling below 22nm

  * How Resistive RAM (ReRAM) works by forming conductive filaments

  * Integrating ReRAM in the back-end-of-line with just two masks

  * Achieving automotive-grade endurance and qualification

  * The future of ReRAM in AI and in-memory compute
