### ASML Says AI Demand Will Outrun Lithography Supply Through 2028

[ASML posted €11.7 billion in Q3 revenue and €5.0 billion net income](https://ml-eu.globenewswire.com/Resource/Download/4beff1ba-33cc-4f21-b926-43b673c9708d), then raised its full-year sales guidance to €44-45 billion as customer inquiries for its lithography tools now stretch to 2030. Demand will exceed the company’s production capacity through at least 2028, and ASML is actively studying further expansion to close that gap. The constraint isn’t theoretical: ASML has already sold out 2027 and 2028 EUV allocations, leaving chipmakers including TSMC, Samsung, and Intel competing for slots on a fixed supply curve. Shares rose on the results. SEMI’s separate forecast puts [global 300mm fab equipment spending at $227 billion by 2029](https://www.semiconductor-digest.com/global-300mm-fab-equipment-spending-to-reach-227-billion-in-2029-as-buildout-accelerates/?utm_source=rss&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=global-300mm-fab-equipment-spending-to-reach-227-billion-in-2029-as-buildout-accelerates), up from $149 billion this year, a trajectory that only tightens the math for any fab operator planning capacity additions.

### Nvidia Locks In AI Chip-Testing Capacity With KYEC Prepayment Deal

King Yuan Electronics has secured an advance payment from Nvidia to fund a [NT$45 billion (roughly $1.4 billion) U.S. chip-testing facility](https://app.dealroom.co/news/note/nvidia-prepays-for-us-chip-testing-capacity-in-kyec-s-1-4b-expansion), with Texas identified as the likely site. The prepayment structure means Nvidia commits capital before a single machine is installed, effectively reserving capacity rather than simply contracting for it. KYEC, one of Taiwan’s leading independent test houses, will use the funds to build out dedicated AI chip-testing infrastructure on American soil. Nvidia has been tightening its grip on the full supply chain for AI hardware, and locking in test capacity this far upstream reflects how tight advanced packaging and verification services have become for leading-edge chips.

> **Vik:** _Same prepay playbook Nvidia ran with Amkor back in July - $1.5B, also upfront. Now it’s the test side. As Rubin’s power draw climbs, burn-in and liquid cooling become the choke point, and KYEC’s in-house burn-in is exactly why Nvidia’s locking them this early. Back-end capacity as scarce as the front._
> 
> **Austin:** _Credit to Nvidia for bolstering the US chip testing ecosystem. Sure, it’s self-serving; that’s good fiduciary responsibility. But they should get credit for the investment as others will benefit too._

### Delaware Jury Finds Arm Breached License, Awards Qualcomm $2

A [Delaware jury found that Arm breached its technology license agreement with Qualcomm](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/qualcomm-says-jury-found-arm-breached-license-agreement-2026-10-13/) while awarding only nominal damages of $2, one dollar on each of two claims. Arm, which had sought to void Qualcomm’s license over its 2021 acquisition of chip startup Nuvia, called the two-dollar figure a vindication of its good-faith conduct and the merits of its position. Qualcomm had countered that Arm wrongfully attempted to strip it of rights it legally acquired. The breach finding hands Qualcomm a legal basis to assert its Nuvia-derived architecture work falls within its existing license, undercutting Arm’s core argument in the dispute. Arm prevailed on four separate claims in the same proceeding.

> **Vik:** _Nominal damages here means the jury found Arm breached but saw no provable dollar harm. Qualcomm wins the principle, not a payout. For two companies this size, two dollars was never the prize._

### SK Group Chairman Chey Tae-won sells $406M stake to Dell family office

SK Group Chairman Chey Tae-won has [sold a $406 million stake in the conglomerate to MSD Partners](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/sk-group-chairman-chey-sells-stake-dell-family-office-fund-406-million-2026-10-14/), the family office of Dell Technologies founder Michael Dell. The transaction gives a U.S. technology-aligned investor a direct financial position in SK Group, the parent of SK Hynix, the world’s leading supplier of high-bandwidth memory for AI accelerators. Terms of the deal were not disclosed beyond the headline figure. MSD Partners manages capital for Dell personally and has a history of technology-sector bets. The sale came from Chey’s personal holdings rather than a corporate treasury transaction, making it a direct transfer of ownership between two of technology’s better-known principals.

> **Austin:** _Some context: SK Inc. said in an Oct 2 filing the sale funds the property division in Chey’s divorce case. So it’s a seller who needs cash, and it’s interesting that Michael Dell’s family office is the one taking the block. Michael Dell is awesome, if anyone should profit off memory, it’s that guy; he and his family will probably turn around it give a ton of it away too._
