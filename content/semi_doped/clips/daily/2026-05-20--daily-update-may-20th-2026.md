### **Dell Tech World brings frontier models on-prem**

At Dell Technologies World 2026 on Monday, CEO Michael Dell **announced on-prem partnerships pulling frontier models** from Google, OpenAI, xAI, Palantir, and Reflection inside customer-owned Dell infrastructure. Dell also unveiled a Desk-Side Agentic AI workstation running 70-billion to 1-trillion parameter models locally. Dell reports 5,000 customers on the Dell AI Factory. ### **NVIDIA ships first Vera CPUs to Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceX, Oracle**

NVIDIA delivered its first Vera CPUs to Anthropic in San Francisco, OpenAI in Mission Bay, and SpaceX in Palo Alto on Friday, followed by a shipment to Oracle Cloud Infrastructure in Santa Clara on Monday, with the units hand-delivered by Ian Buck, Nvidia’s VP of hyperscale and high-performance computing. The deliveries land the same week NVIDIA reports first-quarter fiscal-2027 earnings after the close. ([Nvidia](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/vera-cpu-delivery/)).

### **Korea floats emergency action as Samsung strike talks resume**

Samsung Electronics and its largest union resumed talks on Monday in a last-ditch effort to avert a strike, as South Korean Prime Minister Kim Min-seok warned that the government could invoke emergency labor mediation if a walkout threatens the national economy. The remarks marked the first official mention of emergency intervention. Micron shares fell as much as 7% on concerns that a work stoppage at the world’s largest DRAM and NAND producer would tighten memory chip supply, while Korean stocks dropped 5% amid the chip slump and broader overheating concerns. SK Hynix has separately been running at full HBM allocation, according to industry reports. ([The Elec](https://www.thelec.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=10532), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-19/korea-s-world-beating-market-rally-hinges-on-a-handful-of-stocks))
