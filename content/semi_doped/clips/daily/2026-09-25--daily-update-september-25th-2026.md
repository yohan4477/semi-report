### Oracle Force Majeure Notice on New Mexico Campus Rattles Power Equipment Suppliers

[Oracle sent a force majeure notice to Blue Owl Capital](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-24/oracle-cites-seeks-cover-from-data-center-delays-video) over the Project Jupiter data center campus being built in New Mexico, citing a series of setbacks that have stalled construction on the Stargate-affiliated site. The move lets Oracle seek cover from contractual obligations tied to the development timeline. Bloom Energy and GE Vernova both saw their shares fall on the news, as investors repriced the revenue visibility each company had built around steady hyperscale construction schedules. Barron’s flagged GE Vernova specifically as exposed, given its power equipment backlog depends on data center builds proceeding on time. [Force majeure claims of this kind can suspend or void supplier delivery commitments](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/oracle-issues-force-majeure-notice-to-blue-owl-following-series-of-setbacks-at-project-jupiter-data-center-campus-report/), leaving infrastructure vendors holding contracted capacity with no clear installation date.

> **Vik:** [Austin Lyons](https://open.substack.com/users/8066776-austin-lyons?utm_source=mentions) _made this really keen observation on the[Semi Doped podcast](https://x.com/semidoped/status/2103111070093291799?s=20), a week before this happened. He was totally right. It’s the local governments who will set the pace of AI. Not Silicon Valley._

### Akamai Commits $11.6 Billion to Anthropic, Crossing $500 Billion Compute Threshold

[Akamai’s multi-year agreement with Anthropic](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-signs-11-6-billion-cloud-deal-with-akamai-pushing-its-compute-spending-past-500-billion-in-under-a-year/) covers $11.6 billion in cloud infrastructure, with Akamai separately committing $5.5 billion in capital expenditure to support the build-out. The deal isn’t structured as a standard cloud contract: forkast.news describes it as an equity position in the model layer, recasting Akamai from a CDN operator into a direct stakeholder in frontier AI. Anthropic’s cumulative compute spending [crossed $500 billion in under a year](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-signs-11-6-billion-cloud-deal-with-akamai-pushing-its-compute-spending-past-500-billion-in-under-a-year/), with Akamai’s commitment pushing it past that threshold. That pace, for a company still privately held, is striking on its own terms. Reuters reports Anthropic is separately seeking 50.1% voting control for co-founders ahead of a planned IPO.

> **Vik:** _This is for their CPUs.**Conspiracy theory** : Anthropic will release a personal agent like Muse._

### Goldman Sees Hyperscaler AI CapEx Hit $1.2 Trillion as Nscale Closes Pre-IPO Round

Goldman Sachs strategists project spending on AI infrastructure by the five largest US hyperscalers will [rise more than 50% in 2027 to $1.2 trillion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-25/goldman-sees-hyperscaler-ai-capex-rising-50-to-1-2-trillion), sustaining semiconductor and power-equipment demand well beyond the current buildout cycle. Against that backdrop, European AI cloud provider Nscale closed a [$3.36 billion pre-IPO convertible note round led by Third Point](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-25/nscale-raises-3-36-billion-in-pre-ipo-round-led-by-third-point), giving the company fresh capital to expand its GPU-dense data center platform ahead of a public listing. The convertible structure lets Nscale defer equity dilution while locking in growth funding now, at a moment when hyperscaler commitments give lenders unusual confidence in forward AI compute demand. Reuters Breakingviews called the implied valuation a stretch, but the capital is closed.

### Google Launches Fridge-Size TPU Satellite Aboard SpaceX Transporter-18

Google’s Project Suncatcher sends its first hardware into orbit next week aboard SpaceX Transporter-18, carrying a fridge-size unit it calls a minimum viable product. The satellite packs [four TPUs and 1,000 watts of solar power](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/data-centers/google-is-blasting-an-experimental-ai-data-center-into-orbit-first-satellite-will-feature-just-four-tensor-processing-units), though overheating constrains each compute run to just 15 minutes despite the spacecraft being rated for a full year of orbital operations. That thermal ceiling is itself a core finding the mission is designed to produce: Google needs real vacuum-and-radiation data before it can engineer around the problem. The payload rides a SpaceX rideshare rather than a dedicated launch, keeping early validation costs in check. No other major hyperscaler has [flown an AI compute payload](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/project-suncatcher-google-to-launch-first-space-data-center-test-in-orbit-next-week/) of this kind, leaving Google alone in collecting radiation-hardened TPU performance data from low Earth orbit.

> **Vik:** _Its cute they sent up a 4-TPU scale-up domain to test in space. I always maintain that launch costs are the biggest impediment to datacenters in space. Watch for drop in launch costs as a leading indicator for space compute._

### ASML Sold Nothing to European Fabs as Dutch PM Eyes China Deal

ASML’s European revenue share has dropped to zero, with the company saying it has [sold “absolutely nothing” in the region](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/asmls-revenue-share-drops-to-zero-percent-in-europe-says-its-sold-absolutely-nothing-in-the-region-this-year/) this year. That stark absence of domestic orders arrives as Dutch Prime Minister Rob Jetten confirmed he has discussed the MATCH Act with President Trump, legislation that would further restrict ASML’s China sales. Jetten told Bloomberg Television he is “pretty sure we come to some agreement that is workable for each and every one,” offering [cautious optimism about the negotiations](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-24/dutch-prime-minister-has-discussed-match-act-with-trump-video). Europe produces none of ASML’s revenue while its own government works to tighten the screws on the one market still buying heavily.

> **Vik:** _That’s because there are no chips happening in Europe. Absolutely nothing too surprising._

### Muse Gives Every User a Free Isolated Linux VM in the Cloud

Meta’s Muse AI assistant now provisions each user with a dedicated Secure VM running a full Ubuntu Linux image, giving users root-level visibility into the runtime cell filesystem. A Sentinel process running outside the runtime cell oversees sensitive actions, while passwords and other secrets are stored separately from the cell. Users can install software, compile code, browse the web, and download their personal agent data via Settings > Data controls > Download your agent data.
