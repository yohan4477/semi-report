We are very excited to announce the launch of WISE Employment Korea and welcome 30+ new members to our growing team!

WISE Employment Korea will be headed up by Country Manager Joo Sung (Jason) Han and will focus on the Korean Government's mainstream employment services contract (NESS under the Ministry of Employment and Labour), as well as some youth services.

WISE Employment Regional Director, Asia, Ary Laufer, said: "WISE is extremely excited to be working with a talented group of staff to bring the WISE vision to Korea. We are looking forward to expanding our social impact for the people and community of Korea".
