🌟 Olive Young Goes Global: Korea's Beauty Retail Giant Takes on the World

Olive Young's growth trajectory has been nothing short of remarkable. From 1.8 trillion KRW ($1.3 billion USD) in revenue in 2020, they've skyrocketed to 4.8 trillion KRW ($3.5 billion USD) in 2024, achieving an operating profit of 600 billion KRW ($440 million USD) with a 12.5% operating margin. This level of profitability is truly impressive for the general retail industry.

Once considered a "white elephant" within the CJ Group, Olive Young's transformation alongside the K-beauty boom has been largely attributed to CJ's advisor Noh Hee-young's branding strategy. 

Today, it's become commonplace to see foreign tourists shopping at Olive Young instead of traditional duty-free stores. And now, Olive Young is finally making its serious push into global markets. They've established subsidiaries in Japan and the US, with store launches planned for LA and Tokyo.

The vision of selling K-beauty products overseas through Korea's proven retail expertise is truly exciting.

I believe this is just the beginning. I genuinely hope to see Olive Young grow into a global brand to become next Sephora! 🇰🇷
